02 Process

Five steps, in order, every account.

Nothing here is proprietary. It's just done consistently, which is the part most teams can't sustain while also closing deals.

  1. 01

    Agree on who counts

    Titles, company size, tech stack, trigger events. We settle what a qualified meeting means before I make a single dial — so nobody argues about lead quality in week three.

  2. 02

    Build the list by hand

    Sourced and verified account by account. Bulk scraped lists burn your sending domain and waste the calls — they don't get used here.

  3. 03

    Work both channels at once

    Email sequences run while I'm on the phone against the same account list. Whichever channel they answer, there's a person on the other end who knows their business.

  4. 04

    Qualify live

    Problem, timing, budget, and decision process confirmed in conversation — not inferred from a form fill. If it doesn't hold up, it doesn't get booked.

  5. 05

    Hand it off clean

    The meeting lands on your rep's calendar with notes attached. They show up, they sell. Weekly report on dials, replies, meetings set, and meetings held.

04 Fit

Worth a conversation, or worth skipping.

I'd rather tell you no in the first call than three months in. Here's the honest split.

Good fit

  • You sell B2B at a deal size that justifies a human conversation.
  • You have a product and paying customers, but no consistent outbound motion.
  • Your closers are strong and simply short on meetings.
  • You need pipeline this quarter, not a six-week hiring process.
  • You can give me 30 minutes a week for feedback on the calls.

Probably not

  • You want a bulk list of scraped contacts to blast yourself.
  • You're selling B2C or transactional low-ticket.
  • Volume matters more to you than fit.
  • Nobody internally can take a meeting within two weeks of it being set.
  • You want leads with no input on who they are.
